REQUIREMENT
-----------
Donors make recurring financial commitments (pledges) that fund individual
missionaries. The platform must track:
  - Donor pledges (recurring commitment: amount + frequency + missionary)
  - Recurring contributions (actual payments against the pledge)
  - Missed payments -> notify donor AND missionary/admin
  - Funding shortages (missionary received < target) -> notify missionary + donors/admin
Disbursement to missionaries (paying them) = adjacent/separate scope (Payroll or
Supplier payment), not part of the core pledge-tracking requirement.

VERDICT
-------
FEASIBLE and a strong fit. Foundation = ERPNext's recurring-billing + receivables
engine (NOT the fundraising module). Split: ~65% configuration / ~35% targeted
custom. 35% (not lower) because of real v16 gotchas (below).

Mental model:
  recurring pledge  = Subscription (auto-generates a periodic invoice)
  contribution      = Payment against the invoice
  missed payment    = Overdue invoice
  funding shortage  = received-vs-target gap per missionary

VERIFIED IN THIS v16 INSTALL (doctypes present)
-----------------------------------------------
  Subscription, Subscription Plan, Dunning, Payment Request, Budget,
  Accounting Dimension, Auto Repeat (frappe), Notification (frappe).
NOT installed: Non-Profit module (Donor / Donation / Membership). Not needed —
the accounting engine is the better fit. (Installable as a separate app if ever wanted.)

CONCEPT -> MODULE MAPPING
-------------------------
  Donor                          -> Customer (group "Donors")                 [Standard]
  Missionary / receiver          -> Accounting Dimension "Missionary" on every
                                    txn + Project/Cost Center per missionary   [Config]
  Recurring pledge ($X/month)    -> Subscription + Subscription Plan           [Standard]
  Contribution received          -> Payment Entry against invoice (-> Paid)     [Standard]
  Missed payment                 -> Invoice auto Overdue past due date          [Standard]
  Reminders / escalation         -> Dunning + Payment Request (online link)     [Config]
  Notify donor & missionary      -> Notification (event/date-based email/SMS)   [Custom routing]
  Funding shortage per missionary-> received-vs-target: custom report + sched.  [Custom]
                                    alert (Budget is EXPENSE-oriented, not income)
  Donor / missionary portal      -> Customer Portal + tailored view            [Light custom]

RECOMMENDED WORKFLOW (end to end)
---------------------------------
  1. Onboard donor as Customer (group "Donors").
  2. Record pledge = Subscription ($100/mo), tagged to a Missionary.
  3. Each period -> Subscription auto-creates an invoice earmarked to that missionary.
  4. Donor pays (Payment Entry, or online via Payment Request) -> invoice Paid.
  5. Unpaid past due -> invoice Overdue -> notify donor + missionary; Dunning escalates.
  6. Compare missionary received vs monthly target; if short -> alert missionary + donors/admin.
  7. Dashboards: pledged vs received, overdue list, shortfall by missionary, donor history.

WHERE REAL CUSTOMIZATION IS NEEDED (Phase 2, ~35%)
--------------------------------------------------
  - Funding-shortage engine: received-vs-target per missionary (Budget can't do income
    shortfall) -> custom report + scheduled script + alerts.
  - Dual-recipient notification routing: resolve/email the correct missionary tied to a
    donor's pledge (needs a resolver, not just a checkbox).
  - "Missionary" link field on Subscription/Customer to earmark cleanly.
  - Donor / missionary self-service portal views.
  - Donor-friendly labels ("Support Commitment" instead of "Invoice").

KEY DESIGN DECISION
-------------------
  Subscription -> Invoice  (RECOMMENDED)  vs  custom "Pledge" doctype.
  Subscription->Invoice reuses overdue detection, dunning, aging, online payment out of
  the box (just relabel "Invoice" as "Support Commitment"). Custom Pledge doctype is
  semantically cleaner but rebuilds the missed-payment/reminder machinery -> more custom/risk.
  => Go with Subscription -> Invoice.

WHY 35% CUSTOM, NOT LESS (v16 gotchas)
--------------------------------------
  - Income-side shortfall is not native (Budget targets expenses).
  - Notification recipient routing needs logic to reach the right missionary.
  - Field/validation quirks: read-only-after-create fields, exact-match validations,
    reference-required rules (all observed on this instance).
  - Caching & desk white-labelling: standard actions hidden, aggressive caching -> "simple
    config" sometimes needs backend follow-up.
  - Portal tailoring is light dev, not pure config.

PHASED PLAN
-----------
  Phase 1 (config, no code): donors as Customers; pledges as Subscriptions (auto-invoicing);
    payment tracking + overdue; Dunning/Payment Request; Accounting Dimension per missionary;
    event/date Notifications to donors; dashboards.
  Phase 2 (targeted custom): funding-shortage engine + alerts; dual-recipient routing;
    Missionary link field; donor/missionary portal; friendly labels.
